Quote Management

Common questions about managing your quotes

How do I create a new quote?

Go to the Quote List page and select the Add Quote button. Choose Commercial Property from the available business types and proceed to start a new quote.

How do I resume an incomplete quote?

Open the Quote List page and find the quote marked as Incomplete. Select the Resume action to continue the quote from the step where you previously stopped.

How do I view a completed quote?

Completed quotes are available in the Quote List. Select the View action for a completed quote to open its details.

Can I delete a quote?

Yes. Quotes can be removed from the Quote List by selecting the Delete action associated with the quote.

How can I identify the status of a quote?

Each quote displays a status in the Quote List. An Incomplete quote can be resumed, while a Completed quote can be viewed through its details.

Commercial Property

Common questions about the Commercial Property application

What information is required for a Commercial Property quote?

The application requires business information, property information, and coverage information. The required details are collected throughout the Commercial Property quote wizard.

What business information is required?

Business information may include the business name, business type, contact person, email, phone number, and business address.

What property information is required?

Property information includes details such as property type, property address, year built, building area, number of floors, construction type, and occupancy type.

Can I save my Commercial Property quote and continue later?

Yes. An incomplete quote can be saved and resumed later. When you return to the Quote List, you can use the Resume action to continue the application.

What happens after I complete the Commercial Property quote?

Once all required steps are completed, the quote becomes Completed. You can then view the completed quote from the Quote List.
